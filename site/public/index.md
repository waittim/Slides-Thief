# Slides Thief — Convert Angled Slide or Document Photos to PDF

> Straighten skewed photos of slides or documents and export them as a clean PDF locally in your browser. No upload required. Supports JPG, PNG, WebP, HEIC, and HEIF.

Detect source boundaries, correct perspective distortion, and create one PDF locally in your browser without uploading source photos.

This is the markdown alternate of <https://slidesthief.com/>. The interactive app is that HTML page.

## Supported images

JPEG, PNG, WebP, HEIC, and HEIF.

## Automatic and manual correction

Detect four corners automatically, then drag any handle that needs adjustment.

## Optional readability enhancement

Keep original colors by default, or choose Clean, High contrast, or Black & white before PDF export.

## CLI and machine-readable reports

The Python CLI supports batch processing, manual correction files, and JSON detection reports. See the [CLI contract](https://slidesthief.com/docs/cli.md).
